Math Tug of War Rules

Overview: Math Tug of War is a two-team, same-screen mathematical battle. Blue Team plays on the left, and Red Team plays on the right.

🎮 How to Play

  • Both teams solve questions independently on their own numeric keypads.
  • Enter your answer using the keypad buttons and click Submit.
  • If you are stuck, you may click Skip (penalty applies if configured).
  • In Timed Mode, each question has an independent timer. If it expires, timeout penalties apply and you advance automatically.

🪢 The Rope & Scoring

  • The central tug-of-war ribbon moves toward the team that currently leads in score.
  • If scores are level, the ribbon stays aligned with the white center line.
  • Unless negative scoring is enabled, team scores cannot fall below 0.

🏆 Winning the Match

  • Instant Victory (Perfect Run): If a team answers all questions with 100% accuracy and NO skips before the opposing team finishes, they win immediately!
  • Otherwise, both teams play until finished, and the team with the highest final score wins. If scores are equal, the match is a draw!
